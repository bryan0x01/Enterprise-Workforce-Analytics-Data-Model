# Recommended Visuals

## Executive Workforce Overview

- Cards: active headcount, total annual compensation, average salary, average tenure, hires, terminations.
- Line chart: monthly active headcount.
- Bar chart: headcount by department.
- Map or bar chart: headcount by location.
- Combo chart: actual headcount versus planned headcount.

## Workforce Movement

- Clustered column chart: hires and terminations by month.
- Line chart: turnover rate by month.
- Bar chart: voluntary versus involuntary terminations.
- Matrix: movement by department and job level.

## Compensation Analysis

- Cards: average salary and total annual compensation.
- Bar chart: average salary by department.
- Bar chart: average salary by job level.
- Column chart: employees by compensation band.
- Table: employees below or above job compensation band.

## Organizational Analysis

- Bar chart: headcount by manager.
- Bar chart: headcount by job family.
- Donut chart: employment type distribution.
- Bar chart: location distribution.
- Matrix: department, job family, and job level.

## Workforce Planning

- Line and column combo chart: actual versus planned headcount.
- Bar chart: headcount variance by department.
- Table: departments above plan.
- Table: departments below plan.
- Card: total hiring demand, using positive plan variance if desired.

## Data Quality

- Cards: total issues, error count, critical count, unresolved issues.
- Bar chart: issues by severity.
- Bar chart: issues by source file.
- Bar chart: issues by validation rule.
- Table: reconciliation checks and statuses.
